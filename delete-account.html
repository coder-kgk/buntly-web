Delete Your Buntly Account

Effective date: August 5, 2026  ·  Last updated: August 9, 2026

You can delete your Buntly account at any time. This page explains how to request deletion and what data is removed.

How to delete your account (in the app)

Follow these steps while signed in to Buntly:

  1. Open Buntly
  2. Tap More
  3. Tap Settings
  4. Scroll to the Delete my account section
  5. Tap Delete my account
  6. Review the confirmation message and tap Start deletion to confirm

After confirmation, your account is deactivated immediately and hidden from the app. You are signed out. Your data is scheduled for permanent purge after 30 days, unless an active legal hold delays purge.

Changed your mind within 30 days?

Deletion does not cancel automatically when you try to sign in. To cancel:

  • Use the cancellation link in your deletion confirmation email, or
  • On the Buntly sign-in screen (when your account shows deletion in progress), tap Cancel my deletion to receive a new cancellation email, then use the link in that email

What gets deleted

When the 30-day period ends (and no legal hold applies), the following data tied to your account is permanently purged from active systems:

  • Dog profiles and care records you own
  • Household caregiver access
  • Account preferences
  • Crisis Companion AI transcripts — hard-deleted

Other AI conversation logs (ai_conversation_log) are anonymized on purge: identity fields are cleared; conversation content and timestamps may be retained as implemented. They are not treated the same as Crisis transcripts.

What may be retained

As described in our Privacy Policy (Section 7), before the auth user is permanently removed we may stamp limited identity evidence onto designated service-role evidence tables (for legal claims, abuse investigation, and repeat-actor detection). As implemented, that stamp may include your full email address, a one-way email hash, email domain, display name (if available), internal user id, and account created/deleted timestamps. Active legal holds can delay purge. Anonymized subscription event aggregates may also remain where identity is stripped.

Full detail: Privacy Policy — Data Retention and Account Deletion.

Can’t access the app?

If you cannot open Buntly or sign in, you can still request account deletion by email:

Email: privacy@buntly.app
Subject line: Account Deletion Request
Include: The email address registered to your Buntly account

We aim to process email deletion requests promptly. Identity verification may be required before we complete the request.
