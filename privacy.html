Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Effective: June 2026

1. Who we are

Buntly (“we,” “us,” “our”) operates the Buntly mobile application and related services. We help dog parents organize health records, receive AI-assisted guidance, and access emergency support tools.

For India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), Buntly acts as a Data Fiduciary. Grievance contact: geethk.kt@gmail.com — we aim to respond within 30 days.

2. Scope

This Policy describes how we handle personal information when you use the Buntly app, create accounts, use AI features, or visit buntly.app.

3. Information we collect

Account

  • Email address and name (if provided)
  • Authentication identifiers and account preferences

Dog profiles

  • Name, breed, age, weight, photos, health conditions, and care notes
  • Household sharing and caregiver settings

Health logs

  • Symptoms, incidents, medications, vet visits, mood, activity, food, water, weight, grooming
  • Emergency preparedness and insurance summaries you enter

AI conversations

  • Messages sent to Crisis Companion and other AI features
  • Symptom selections and dog context used to generate responses

Device & usage

  • Device ID, OS version, app version, crash diagnostics
  • Feature usage and app interactions (via PostHog, generally anonymized)
  • Push notification tokens and subscription status (RevenueCat)

Location

We access location only during Emergency Vet Finder search to show nearby clinics. We do not store location history as a standing profile field.

4. How we use data

  • Provide AI health guidance and Crisis Companion emergency support
  • Operate dog profiles, health logs, and household sharing
  • Send medication and care reminders you enable
  • Analyze health trends from data you log
  • Improve app reliability, safety, and support
  • Process subscriptions and comply with law

5. AI disclosure

Claude AI (Anthropic) processes health queries. The AI is not a veterinarian. AI guidance does not replace professional vet care. In emergencies, contact a vet immediately. AI may make errors. Conversations may be reviewed for safety and may be retained up to 90 days.

7. India — DPDP compliance

As a Data Principal in India, you may have rights to access, correction, erasure, and grievance redressal. You may withdraw consent for optional processing via Settings or email. Contact our Grievance Officer at geethk.kt@gmail.com.

8. United States — CCPA / CPRA

California and similar US state residents may have rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale (we do not sell data), plus non-discrimination for exercising rights. Email geethk.kt@gmail.com with subject “Privacy Request.”

9. GDPR (baseline)

Where GDPR applies, you may have access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection rights. Contact us or your supervisory authority.

10. Data retention

  • Account & health logs: until deletion requested
  • AI conversations: 90 days
  • Deleted accounts: 30-day recovery window, then purged from active systems

11. Children’s privacy

Buntly is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Contact us to request deletion if you believe we have.

12. Security & changes

We use encryption, access controls, and monitoring appropriate to health data. We may update this Policy at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
