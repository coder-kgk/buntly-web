Section 2 — Definitions

“Personal Data” / “Personal Information” means any information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to an identifiable individual (you), including account information, device identifiers, and information you provide about yourself or your dog when it can be linked to you.

“Sensitive Personal Data” means personal data requiring heightened protection under applicable law, including: health and medical information about your dog that you log in the app; symptoms, diagnoses, medications, and vet records; and AI conversations about health, emergencies, or medical concerns.

“Data Fiduciary” (India DPDP Act, 2023) means the entity that determines the purpose and means of processing personal data — that entity is Buntly.

“Data Principal” (India DPDP Act, 2023) means you — the individual to whom the personal data relates.

“AI Features” means Claude-powered functionality in Buntly, including Crisis Companion, breed information, health guidance, vet prep summaries, insurance explainers, and related AI-assisted tools.

“Processing” means any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, transmission, deletion, or anonymization.

Section 4 — Artificial Intelligence Disclosure

4.1 AI Provider

Buntly uses Claude, developed by Anthropic PBC, 799 Market Street, San Francisco, CA 94103, USA (“Anthropic”) to power AI features.

4.2 Data Sent to Anthropic

When you use any AI feature, the following may be transmitted to Anthropic’s servers in the United States:

  • ✓ Your message text
  • ✓ Your dog’s name (if included in message)
  • ✓ Health symptoms and conditions you describe
  • ✓ Context from your dog’s profile (if enabled)
  • ✗ Your email address (NOT sent)
  • ✗ Your payment information (NOT sent)
  • ✗ Your precise location (NOT sent)

By using AI features, you explicitly consent to this international transfer of data to the USA.

Anthropic’s Privacy Policy: anthropic.com/privacy
Anthropic’s Usage Policy: anthropic.com/legal/usage-policy

4.3 Critical Limitations — Read Before Using AI Features

The AI in Buntly is NOT a veterinarian.
AI responses are NOT veterinary advice.
AI responses are NOT medical advice.
AI responses do NOT create any veterinarian-client-patient relationship.

  • The AI may produce incorrect, incomplete, or outdated information
  • The AI cannot physically examine your dog
  • The AI cannot order diagnostic tests
  • The AI cannot prescribe medications
  • The AI does not have access to your dog’s complete medical history unless you provide it in the conversation
  • AI responses are generated probabilistically and may differ between sessions

IN ANY EMERGENCY: CALL YOUR VETERINARIAN OR EMERGENCY ANIMAL HOSPITAL IMMEDIATELY.
DO NOT RELY ON AI RESPONSES IN LIFE-THREATENING SITUATIONS.

4.4 AI Conversation Review

Buntly’s safety team may review AI conversations that are:

  • Flagged by automated safety systems
  • Reported by users
  • Randomly sampled for quality assurance (< 1% of conversations)

All reviews are conducted under strict confidentiality obligations.

4.5 AI Accuracy Disclaimer

Buntly makes no warranty that AI responses are accurate, complete, current, or suitable for any particular purpose. Users rely on AI responses at their own risk.

Section 10 — Security

We implement:

  • TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest (via Supabase)
  • Row-level security on all database tables
  • Two-factor authentication for all admin access
  • Regular security audits
  • Jailbreak/root detection on mobile app
  • Secure token storage (device keychain)

No system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach affecting your rights, we will notify you within 72 hours (GDPR standard) and within the timeframe required by applicable law.
